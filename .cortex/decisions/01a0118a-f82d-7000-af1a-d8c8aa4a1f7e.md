ensureProject was a check-then-insert with nothing to serialize it and no constraint behind it, so unlike a migration race it did not even error: two processes opening the same repository at once created two project nodes for one canonical id, and sessions then split their BELONGS_TO edges across two identities with nothing to merge them back.

Two changes, both wanted. Migration 006 collapses any duplicates that already exist onto the oldest row — repointing their edges first — and adds a unique index on nodes(title) partial to kind = 'project', so a second identity cannot be written by any path. It is partial because only a project's title is an identity: two decisions may legitimately share one, and a project with no title keeps SQL's distinct NULLs. ensureProject then wraps its insert in an immediate transaction with the lookup repeated inside, while the first lookup stays outside and unlocked because it answers on all but the very first call of a store's life.
