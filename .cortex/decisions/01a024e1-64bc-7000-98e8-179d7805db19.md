release.yml restated a subset of ci.yml — typecheck, `bun test`, smoke:compiled — while README claimed it "re-runs the gate". Missing: lint:ci, the 100% coverage threshold, coverage:audit, and the install.sh/target.ts suffix-agreement check, which is the one thing standing between a suffix rename and a fleet that cannot upgrade.

ci.yml gained an `on: workflow_call` trigger and release.yml now calls it (`jobs.gate.uses: ./.github/workflows/ci.yml`), so a job added to CI cannot be forgotten by the release. Copying the missing steps into release.yml was rejected for the obvious reason: it is the same duplication that produced the drift.

The tag-vs-package.json assert stays a separate job (`tag`) that the gate depends on, so a mismatched tag fails in seconds instead of after the full suite.

tests/docs/release-workflow.test.ts parses both YAML files and asserts the call, the needs chain and the absence of restated steps.
