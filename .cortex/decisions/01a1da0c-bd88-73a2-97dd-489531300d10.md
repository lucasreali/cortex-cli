01a01f5b-6ef9 let an installed binary take CORTEX_RELEASE_BASE_URL for any https address on github.com. That still let a fork's release, with a checksums.txt of its own, replace the binary that runs on every prompt. 01a1da0c-3970 replaces the rule: only github.com/lucasreali/cortex-cli is accepted, and the asset must carry a build-provenance attestation from this repository's release workflow. The source-checkout test seam described in 01a01f5b is unchanged and lives on in the new decision.
