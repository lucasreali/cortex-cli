bun run eval:search returned 0 whatever the recall, and no CI job ran it, so it could only ever be read by a human who happened to run it. Every unit test around search asserts ordering over three-decision fixtures, which means a change to the fusion weights, the 0.3 similarity threshold, intentTerms or the FTS tokenizer could halve recall@5 over a real corpus with the whole suite still green.

tests/evaluation/runner.ts takes --min-recall <fraction> and exits 1 when the "current" strategy's mean recall@5 falls below it. Without the flag it still exits 0, so running the eval by hand to read the table stays what it was.

CI runs it in the binary job rather than a job of its own, because that job already downloads the model for smoke:compiled; a separate job would mean a second cold 300 MB fetch. The step builds the store from the committed decision files (cortex sync), embeds it (cortex embed --missing), then asserts the floor.

The floor is 0.85 against 0.917 measured today. It is set to catch a regression, not to ratchet: the corpus grows every time this repo dogfoods a decision, and a floor at the current number would fail on the next decision saved without a ground-truth case.
