init writes `/.cortex/*` followed by the negations `!/.cortex/config` and `!/.cortex/decisions/`: decision files are the product and travel with the code, the config pins the embedding model for every teammate, and decisions.db, code.db and .cortex/local/ stay per-machine because each is rebuilt or local by design. This replaces the earlier rule that ignored the whole directory and kept decisions.db as the local store — that predates decisions-as-files and would now hide the product from git. Rejected: ignoring nothing (the caches are machine-specific and churn on every command).
